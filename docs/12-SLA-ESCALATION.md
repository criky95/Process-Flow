# SLA and Escalation

Cada tarea puede tener:

- expectedDuration
- maximumDuration
- warningThreshold

## States

- ON_TRACK
- AT_RISK
- OVERDUE

## Business calendar

Los tiempos deben poder respetar:

- working hours
- weekends
- holidays

## Escalation

Ejemplo:

```text
80% SLA
→ participante

100%
→ supervisor

120%
→ process owner
```
